How we cook

Three things decide how a plate tastes here, and none of them are shortcuts.

01

Live charcoal

One grill, lump charcoal, no gas assist. The kitchen runs it from open to close so the bed is always at temperature.

02

Bought that morning

Vegetables come from Russell Market, fish from the coast twice a week. If it did not arrive, it is not on the board that day.

03

Short menu, on purpose

Around thirty dishes. Small enough that every one of them gets tasted and adjusted before service.
